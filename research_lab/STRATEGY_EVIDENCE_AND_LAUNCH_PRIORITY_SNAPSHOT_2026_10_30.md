## 1 November 2026 addendum — first executable HIGH STOP horizon, not full-window CAGR

This is a **reanalysis of already studied retrospective adjusted closes**, not new independent evidence or broker-executable returns. **Interpretation:** In two cohorts, the model remains in cash for 1,638 completed closes **strictly after** the modelled STOP execution close (which remains market-exposed until that close); full-window CAGR is therefore *not* a continuously invested active-strategy return. A first STOP on the last historical close has **no invented following-close execution or matched benchmark exit**. The matched 95%-ACWI passive reference is **not a risk-equivalent compliant portfolio** under a literal 50% single-ETF ticker ceiling; it is only a costed, aligned-horizon opportunity-cost comparison. The +17.33pp 2014 outcome cannot be treated as proof of persistent alpha: inception and crisis regime differ, execution is modelled, prices are adjusted-close retrospective, and the source has been repeatedly inspected.
