## Common lessons to adapt, NOT copy

**Warren Buffett / business owners: quality and capital discipline.**
[1986 Berkshire shareholder letter](https://www.berkshirehathaway.com/letters/1986.html) explicitly distinguishes *owner earnings* from reported accounting profit after capital expenditure and working-capital needs. First ask what a business can sustainably distribute; then compare that with a genuinely timestamped price paid. Our original six P4E issuer GAAP scores and P4G MSFT/KO/PG list do NOT measure owner earnings or fair value. Don't reverse-engineer a Buffett label onto them. The proposed stock **V6-Q1** is a NEW, source-dependent owner-earnings-quality-and-valuation hypothesis, not a retroactive validation of P4G.

**Robert Novy-Marx and Cliff Asness / systematic factors.**
[Novy-Marx (2013), Gross Profitability Premium](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jfineco.2013.01.003) and [Asness, Moskowitz and Pedersen (2013), Value and Momentum Everywhere](https://doi.org/10.1111/jofi.12021) justify examining different drivers rather than one-score price chasing. They do **not** license random factor weights or quality labels without historical holding identities. Stock factor Q1 and prospective ETF underlying-company factor S2 have separate sources, comparators, timestamp and fail conditions.

**Moskowitz, Ooi, Pedersen / long-run trend.**
[Time Series Momentum (2012)](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jfineco.2011.11.003) shows historical trend in futures across markets, but V6-R1 weekly ETF rotation, R2 slow ETF rotation and crypto C1 were independently rejected by our cost/turnover/benchmark gates. Never declare one of those successful because its paper ancestry resembles this literature. Trend can be considered as a distinct conditional factor ONLY alongside a source-valid economic signal, not a license to optimize lookbacks on the familiar Fund-100 2014–2026 price series.

**Koijen, Moskowitz, Pedersen, Vrugt / carry.**
[Carry (2018)](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jfineco.2017.11.002) motivates measuring expected roll/financing premia separately from spot price appreciation. Commodity candidate **C10** first needs original dated futures contract-by-contract settlements and the actual ETF roll / holdings schedule, not USO or DBC price chart as invented "carry." Current **$130.30** unleveraged capital cannot support direct CME crude-oil futures. FX1 similarly needs real forward points AND executable financing spreads.

**Cochrane and Piazzesi / fixed income.**
[Bond Risk Premia (2005)](https://doi.org/10.1257/0002828053828581) demonstrates historical information in particular forward-rate combinations. Bond **B3** will qualify point-in-time U.S. yield curve vintages and real-duration ETF exposure before attempting a dated hypothesis. Existing bond price-only and macro-regime rules lagged SHY after costs; preserve those failures. A published term-premium predictor isn't the same as a retail SHY/IEF/TLT alpha system.

**Liu and Tsyvinski / crypto market-specific information.**
[Risks and Returns of Cryptocurrency (2021)](https://doi.org/10.1093/rfs/hhaa113) motivates researching adoption/network indicators and crypto-specific return dynamics. **CR2** must source real original time-stamped network indicators, guard revisions, and beat passive BTC / passive 65/35 BTC/ETH after venue fees. Existing C1 lost substantially on annualized return relative to passive on the original 2020 onward data while lowering drawdown; its turnover exceeded the frozen limit. No 24/7 LIVE crypto permissions currently exist.
