## Deliberate *do not* assumptions

- **Legal shareholder entitlement is not an executed market sale.**
  An ordinary eligible share held through completion gives a
  USD 54.20 cash right. Excluded shares, appraisal claims,
  derivatives, convertibles and employee awards may have DIFFERENT
  treatment; no individual account holding or claimant verified.
- **The receipt/settlement date is unknown.** Do not assume the
  holder could reinvest USD 54.20 on Oct 27/28, nor that the latest
  observed TWTR closing price equals the merger consideration.
  The SEC original 8-K is available ex post for accounting; its
  later original publication is NOT a tradable Oct 27 advance signal.
- No assumed trade/portfolio return, no 2022 point-in-time universe,
  no delisting-complete benchmark, no inferred broker cash balance.
  The four cherry-picked samples and vendor-adjusted closes cannot
  qualify any return-generating Fund-100 strategy.

  restrictions: only tiny research JSON aggregate and hashes
