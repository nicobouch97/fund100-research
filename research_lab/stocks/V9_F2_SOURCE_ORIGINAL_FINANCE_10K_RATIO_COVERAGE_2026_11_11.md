# V9-F2 — source-dated financial-company accounting coverage, never trading alpha

**11 November 2026. Research-only, original own-10-K comparison.**

## Motivation and observed scope

The original independent 10 November V9 source archived 32 SHA256-preselected
present-day SEC issuer CIKs from a current 5,027-CIK association universe.
Only **9 of 32** qualified for the existing generic industrial revenue,
operating-income, operating-cash-flow and capex quality screen.
**8 of 32** had current SEC SIC in the broad finance/insurance/real estate
range, and **6 of those 8** had matching original same-two-10-K US-GAAP
Assets, StockholdersEquity and NetIncomeLoss facts. These are official SEC
*source* observations, not verified traded common classes, historic full
universe, or bank/insurance/REIT risk models.

V9-F2 reuses the verified original identities, original 10-K accession dates,
two-weekday filing lag and exact own-accession USD facts. It computes only
**categorical unranked descriptions**: latest fiscal-year net income divided
by mean of two year-end total assets (a rough ROAA diagnostic), latest
equity divided by latest assets, and whether both fiscal years have positive
net income. Results are aggregated by SEC *current coarse SIC subtype*
without exporting original issuer CIKs, financial amounts or rankings.
Reported margins are NOT compared across banks, brokers, insurers or real
estate models. Both original assets must be positive to calculate any ratio.

SEC SIC code 6770 blank-check vehicles and 6798 REITs are specifically
excluded from generic finance ratio feature output. Bank capital adequacy,
loan quality, liquidity, deposit structure, insurer reserves, and REIT FFO
remain unmeasured. If these source-specific inputs are unavailable, an
issuer's missing generic industrial score is **not a negative grade**.

Every fact must be present in the associated original 10-K, current
FY evidence is accepted only after a conservative two-weekday source lag,
and original 10-K fiscal years must be consecutive and fresh. Non-identical
duplicate amounts, amended duplicate fiscal years, stale or missing
archives, altered issuer identity, wrong balance-sheet versus full-year
fact shape, nonfinite source values and mismatched SIC are rejected,
not silently imputed. Even a source-positive bucket proves no
historical point-in-time research vintage independent of current SEC API
revision. V9's current original source bytes are not yet independently
archived as a source-replayable bundle; aggregate hashes are retained.

## Meaning for strategy

A source ratio is **not** a predictive equity model. Value requires
verified security share class, original shares and close/market cap, issuer
financial semantics, cash flow/debt, issuer-specific accounting and
delisting-aware historical comparison. Profit quality for deposit lenders
must evaluate credit losses and capital; insurers and REITs need their own
metric sets. Use follow-on strictly preregistered sector-specific screens
and costed out-of-sample ACWI/SPY comparisons, without overfitting to
these 32 sample outcomes. Do not need months of paper observation before
researching a genuinely sourced small funded strategy.
