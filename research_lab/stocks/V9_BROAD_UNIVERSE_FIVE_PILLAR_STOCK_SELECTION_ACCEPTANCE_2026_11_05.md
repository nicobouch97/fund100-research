# Fund-100 — broad-universe five-pillar stock strategy acceptance criteria
**Decision registered 5 November 2026 | Research roadmap, not a LIVE release**

The owner confirmed that the eventual autonomous individual-stock strategy must be able to evaluate **valuation, business quality, future prospects, market expectations, and entry timing**, and search a **genuinely broad investable stock universe** so a compelling opportunity need not be confined to 8, 24, or 100 preselected survivors. The owner also supports algorithmic screening, portfolio allocation, and cost-sensitive execution. This is a **future capability requirement**, not a request to switch the launch-first ETF experiment, implement a speculative model immediately, buy licensed data, or unlock trading. Treat this as an acceptance contract for later research, not evidence of investment alpha.

## A. Broad, real, point-in-time stock universe comes FIRST

1. **Target coverage:** Evaluate **thousands of historically eligible US-listed common-share classes** at each eligible date when an approved security-master/data subscription permits. For the first broad-universe validation, aim for at least **1,000 independently identified and price-covered eligible stocks across at least 10 sectors**, not just a hand-picked megacap basket, and record whether these coverage targets were actually met. These are *research coverage targets*, not invented measured counts, not guarantees of market access, and not justification to silently reduce eligibility if missing data. Report counts and sector/size/liquidity distributions on every as-of date.
2. Build the candidate universe from **then-listed securities**, including later delisted, acquired, bankrupt, renamed, and ticker-reused shares. Use permanent **security/share-class identifiers**; today's ticker list, current index constituents, and current successful companies are NOT permitted as a retroactive historical universe. Filter share class, geography, exchange, historical availability, corporate actions, trading status, adjusted/raw price, volume, spread, and broker fractional/order availability **as of** each proposed trade date.
3. Store **actual, licensed historical data** outside the public execution repository; separate entitlement and provenance from research. Treat historical SEC filings as available **only after their original public filing**, and analyst/revision/market-expectation inputs as available **only after their stamped publication**. Do not infer past analyst consensus from a current snapshot. A free current-Dow sample, present-day 24-stock SEC selection, or missing delisting terminal values cannot certify a broad strategy.
4. **Coverage refusal:** If the historical investable set, point-in-time corporate actions, or essential factor fields cannot be independently authenticated, mark the strategy's evidence as *UNQUALIFIED*; do not silently backfill current data or claim a whole-market scan. A deficient or expensive data feed should not block the independent small ETF pilot.
5. **No compulsory purchase:** Screen broadly, then rank and invest only in names passing signal, risk, liquidity and after-cost expected-benefit thresholds; otherwise keep available cash or eligible lower-risk exposures. Large universe coverage does not imply all candidates merit buying.

## B. Each of the FIVE pillars must have an independently testable role

| Pillar | As-of inputs / algorithmic assessment | Failure mode to address |
|---|---|---|
| **1. Valuation / margin of safety** | Sector-appropriate free-cash-flow yield, earnings yield, enterprise value/EBIT, owner earnings, balance sheet and conservative scenario valuation (growth/discount/capital intensity); compare to truly dated sector/size peers and a range of fair values, not one artificial precise target. | Low P/E or fallen price can reflect permanent impairment, leverage, cyclical peak earnings, or false analyst assumptions. |
| **2. Business quality** | Return on invested capital / assets, gross and operating margins, cash conversion, earnings quality/accruals, debt coverage, dilution, capital allocation, resilience, and original-filing consistency. | Exceptional recent margins may be unsustainable; quality filters built from restated future SEC figures create lookahead. |
| **3. Future prospects** | Explicitly dated multi-scenario revenue/margin/free-cash-flow trajectories; industry/competitive structure, reinvestment needs, catalysts and balance-sheet downside; quantify uncertainty, not unearned forecasts of certainty. | Overconfident growth projections or extrapolation of transitory earnings. |
| **4. Market expectations / mispricing** | Where licensable and timestamped, consensus forecasts and *revisions*, implied growth required by the current price, earnings surprise, valuation versus market-implied scenarios, disagreement and event dates. Distinguish undervaluation relative to expectations from simply high observed growth. | Analysts' current/revised future numbers masquerading as historical expectations; inaccessible consensus must be flagged as MISSING, not 0. |
| **5. Entry timing** | Medium-term momentum and relative strength, price/volume/liquidity regime, event timing, volatility/risk, turnover and available expected upside **after spread, fees and slippage**. Timing is a risk/execution overlay, not retroactive permission to cherry-pick bottoms. | Value traps, transaction costs, noisy short-term reversal, and churning before a genuine catalyst. |

The research engine must distinguish **cheap stock**, **undervalued quality business**, and **cheap for a sound reason**, including negative cases. A discount is an uncertain estimate; credible probability-weighted *after-cost* expected returns and downside must drive decisions rather than an arbitrary low multiple. Pillar data may be unobservable for an issuer; track completeness and refuse unsupported claims rather than pretending perfect five-dimensional coverage.

## C. Algorithmic strategy and learning

- Predeclare baseline transparent factor scores and scenario valuation, then compare additive / nonlinear combinations **out of sample**. Machine learning is an optional competitor, not a promotion rule: use purged, chronological rolling training/validation/test with sector and time leakage checks, realistic turnover/cost/capacity, and **never train on outcomes from the reported held-out period**.
- Evaluate the *entire decision pipeline*: as-of broad screen → fundamental quality and valuation → growth/market-expectation assessment → timing and liquidity → competing candidate ranking → **portfolio-wide** sizing, concentration/sector exposure and correlated downside → cost-aware order eligibility → read-back and ongoing monitoring/replacement. A “best stock” is not automatically the best incremental portfolio trade.
- Compare at matched equity/sector/size/risk exposure to **ACWI/SPY, sector peers, and simple transparent factor alternatives**, net of realistic transaction costs, cash financing and any applicable taxes. Report failed eras, adverse event cohorts, coverage gaps, delisted outcomes and market capacity. Require a stable, materially useful *incremental* contribution from each pillar and any ML enhancement before promoting it.
- Strategies compete for the **same fund capital** and approved risk budget (not separate additive sleeves). As capital scales, liquidity, turnover and fractional trading constraints must be recalculated. Use automated research and model reassessment, but prohibit blind live retraining or automatic parameter promotion without precommitted independent evidence.
- After any legitimate standing LIVE release, the user should need **only risk tier and STOP**. Portfolio allocation and rebalance decisions should be automatic inside the mandate; no per-asset, per-trade or recurring owner approval.

## D. Measurable stage gates — no fictional implementation claims

1. **Source availability:** licensed security master with delisted names, as-of original fundamentals, point-in-time analyst expectations if used, corporate actions and tradable prices; legal use/storage confirmed. The 2 November [historical-source acquisition decision](P4_HISTORICAL_DELISTED_DATA_SOURCE_DECISION_2026_11_02.md) explicitly says the full qualified dataset is **not acquired**.
2. **Breadth evidence:** dated per-session eligible/unqualified/selected counts, issuer and share-class identity, capitalisation buckets, ≥10-sector target, delisted names in former cohorts; documented missing-data rates. Never use the present 24-issuer SEC cohort as a substitute for a thousand-name historical backtest.
3. **Five-pillar tests:** each has as-of provenance, explicit missing/unreliable flags, an economically motivated hypothesis and adversarial “looks cheap but is impaired” tests. If forward expectations cannot be authentically sourced, return **EXPECTATIONS_UNQUALIFIED** rather than auto-filling a lookahead model.
4. **Economic and statistical review:** locked historical/forward entry rules, genuinely unselected eras, benchmark/risk matched after-cost relative returns, turnover and capacity, drawdown, concentration, significance uncertainty and limits. Reject a new complexity layer that cannot outperform its simpler baseline robustly.
5. **Portfolio and execution integration:** map only validated, broker-eligible names to the **existing account-wide** LOW/MEDIUM/HIGH limits, fractional and minimum-ticket economics, signed decision/intent/receipt/fill/settled-cash recovery and emergency STOP. **Do not widen the current LIVE order whitelist, capital ceiling or source release switches** on the strength of this document.

## Priority and explicit nonactions

**P0 remains the real, small, risk-bounded V6 ETF pilot**: original ACWI migration, genuine broker settlement, and recurring autonomous controller. This broad-stock research is a **parallel P1 capability**; do not wait 21/63 future sessions to start the separate legitimate ETF pilot. Address actual historical security/master/data entitlement before producing a mock comprehensive screener. No paid subscription, trade, broker POST, private-state CAS or operator release is authorised here.
