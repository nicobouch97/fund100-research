# P4Q — SEC original-accession annual chronology (24 October 2026)

## Why this research is necessary

P4N obtained current official SEC companyfacts and submissions for 16 additional members of the contemporary 24-stock research roster. Twelve have industrial issuer-specific annual GAAP mappings. The prior analysis only reported the latest 10-K. A historical quality-plus-momentum study would otherwise risk using a 2026 accounting fact to explain a decision supposedly made in 2024 or 2025.

P4Q adds an **accession-linked historical annual replay** inside the existing P4N acquisition workflow. It is a source-coverage audit, not a new trading strategy or a separate automated prototype.

## Source and causality rules

- Verify the P4N original 16-issuer archive, original ticker/CIK registry hash, 32 raw SEC source hashes/sizes, exact official URLs, issuer identity, original latest-quality report and no-order status before any replay.
- For each of the 12 industrial issuers, enumerate distinct eligible 10-K conservative availability dates from the actual SEC submissions index. Only original 10-K filings available by each decision date are eligible. Apply the existing two-business-day publication lag; this does **not** prove actual intraday dissemination or US-market holiday timing.
- Reuse the unchanged P4N issuer-specific accounting tags and same-accession current/comparative annual amounts. Later filings' comparative restatements cannot overwrite an earlier accession's quality observation.
- Preserve unavailable annual scores as missing. Do not invent a price, EPS, historical membership, valuation, return, delisting, analyst estimate or source vintage.
- Keep CVX, JPM, LLY and NEE separate: their bank/sector accounting diagnostics are not interchangeable with the 12 industrial quality scores.

The output records per-issuer dates, original 10-K accession, conservative availability, missing/available quality scores and exact original archive SHA-256. It is uploaded into the **existing** P4N SEC artifact alongside the source files. A failure to verify any source stops the entire replay.

## Strict limitations and next research gate

The SEC companyfacts files are retrieved **today** and are not independently archived original historical API snapshots. The selected 24-stock roster itself was chosen with October 2026 knowledge; old delisted companies and historic sector classifications are not reconstructed. These scores are not sector-neutral or valuation-aware, and there is no original corporate-action-complete total-return history. Consequently P4Q does **not** make a historical stock backtest investable, establish alpha or justify a LIVE stock strategy.

A useful next research step is to inspect how many genuinely historical annual accessions survived the current SEC index, then obtain independent historical company-universe, market-cap/valuation and corporate-action data before evaluating quality-versus-momentum decisions. Do not optimise a new rule on the already studied May–October 2026 24-stock roster.

No broker credentials, PAPER orders, LIVE orders, private-state writes, holdings sales, risk-policy modifications or execution releases are involved. The original $130.30 ceiling, operator STOP, ENGAGED kill switch and all LIVE release locks remain unchanged.
