# P4L — 24-stock contemporary source expansion (24 October 2026)

## Decision and economic purpose

The original P4G quality × momentum experiment is frozen to eight
2026 issuers. Its small survivor-selected roster cannot support credible
broad stock-selection conclusions. This extension **does not alter P4G**:
it registers 24 contemporary large US-listed companies spanning technology,
communication, consumer, healthcare, energy, financials, industrials and
utilities. The original eight remain identifiable in the larger roster.

The first source snapshot may use the already completed 23 October close
captured on 24 October, strictly as a **preselection reference**.
It is not a post-freeze investment return. Future dated captures are
retained as original artifacts to permit later independently scored
post-selection comparisons. The cohort itself was selected with 2026
knowledge and is **not** a historical index membership reconstruction.

## Fixed, not retrospectively optimized

Research-only 126-session absolute momentum must be positive and 63-session
SPY-relative momentum must be positive. Rank by relative momentum, tie-break
by ticker, select up to six names, no more than two per *contemporary*
sector. Sector classifications are present-day descriptions, not historical
industry point-in-time proof. SPY is a benchmark, SHY a cash-like reference
price; neither is automatically traded. No quality scores are invented for
the 16 additional companies. The old eight-company P4E scores are NOT
reassigned or extrapolated to new issuers.

The original Yahoo-adjusted daily closes are validated for every expected
NYSE session, finite/positive prices and >45% suspicious gaps. Each full
download and signal is archived with an acquisition UTC timestamp and
canonical SHA-256. Vendor-adjusted closes can be revised, corporate actions
are not independently reconstructed, and Yahoo is not an authenticated
point-in-time source. Missing series or sessions cause a failed capture,
not forward-filled fabricated data.

## Current promotion status and next decisive research

This is **source coverage expansion**, not proof of momentum alpha,
a survivorship-safe backtest, an executable signal, or a claim of returns.
Do not use earlier prices to simulate trading this newly selected roster
before 24 October 2026. A subsequent stage should verify source vintages,
delistings/corporate actions, issuer-level current SEC quality for new names,
and forward costed performance against SPY and a broad equal-weight control.
Do not optimize lookbacks on the same observed returns. No trading strategy
is promoted from this dataset without independent evidence.

This workflow has **no broker credentials, POST endpoints or account access**.
All capital ceilings, operator STOP, environment kill switch and LIVE release
locks are unchanged. No funded or PAPER stock orders are authorised.
