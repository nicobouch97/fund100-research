## Source papers and what they do **not** establish

- Asness, Moskowitz & Pedersen (2013), *Value and Momentum Everywhere*, Journal of Finance: https://doi.org/10.1111/jofi.12021 — reports value and momentum evidence across assets. Does NOT prove MTUM/QUAL is a profitable market-timing rotation.
- Fama & French (2015), *A Five-Factor Asset Pricing Model*, Journal of Financial Economics: https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jfineco.2014.10.010 — a model of size, value, profitability and investment factors; not an ETF selection or trading guarantee.
- Novy-Marx (2013), *The Other Side of Value: The Gross Profitability Premium*, Journal of Financial Economics / NBER: https://doi.org/10.3386/w15940 — firm profitability evidence; QUAL's ETF screening methodology is not gross profitability-to-assets.
- Hurst, Ooi & Pedersen (2017), *A Century of Evidence on Trend-Following Investing*: https://doi.org/10.3905/jpm.2017.44.1.015 — time-series trend evidence, predominantly including derivatives. It does not guarantee a long-only small-cash-account equivalent.
- Core archived data source is the **already existing** `fund100_v7_listed_factor_etf_returns_v1_0.py` original MTUM/QUAL/SPY/ACWI public-adjusted-price research. Yahoo public historical bars can revise, MTUM index rules changed in November 2020, source is not delisting-complete constituent data, venue eligibility/spread/fractional quantities remain unverified.

The quarter-end schedule, 47.5/47.5 weights and 5% cash are **constraints-driven starting benchmarks, not performance-optimised coefficients**.
