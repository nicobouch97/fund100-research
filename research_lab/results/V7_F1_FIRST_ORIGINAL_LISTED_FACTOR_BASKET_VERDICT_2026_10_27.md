## Actual investment decision

**HOLD / DO NOT PROMOTE F1 AS THE NEW AUTOMATED LIVE CHAMPION.** It underperformed the matched SPY buy-and-hold benchmark in two of three preselected historical windows after cost; although it outperformed ACWI in all three, there is insufficient evidence that this precise allocation or quarterly schedule reliably adds net future return. It also concentrates fully in correlated US factor equities and does not diversify broader equity market loss. Stock and ETF price series have vendor revision risk; this is not a prospective independently observed alpha track record.

**Keep literature-first priority**, not this exact portfolio as a guaranteed winner: momentum and genuine company profitability remain plausible components of a diverse investment process. The user explicitly wants faster implementation using independently published factor research rather than building excessive bespoke SEC discovery infrastructure. Use existing prospective direct-stock momentum/quality P4G/P4L/M1 and simple eligible factor vehicles as non-trading controls; promote only if the expected after-cost improvement and funding/account risk justification is credible, not simply because a descriptive history looks favorable. Our frozen 24-company direct-stock roster lacks broad authenticated quality values, so do not call it a completed 24-stock quality×momentum strategy.
