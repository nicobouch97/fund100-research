## Registered size-only model

Future **illustrative** funded equity tiers are $130.30, $200.00, $200.70, $250.00, $500.00, $1,000.00 and $5,000.00. For EACH tier compare (1) current actual principal authorisation ceiling fixed at $130.30 regardless of additional deposits and (2) a clearly-labelled **hypothetical separately approved matching ceiling**. Neither state asserts that a deposit has occurred. Actual deployable principal in any future calculation must be the lower of *authenticated account funds* and *explicit versioned allowed principal*. Do not conflate profit appreciation and automatically usable additional deposits.

Unchanged purely illustrative 75% diversified U.S.-equities / 5% BTC / 5% GLD / 15% cash proposal retains XLK 37.5%, XLE 22.5%, IWM 15%; does **not** claim the legacy 14-ETF portfolio has these weights. This is not an optimised or finally approved allocation and must be revisited as the best strategy evolves and funding grows.

Budget each new purchase against its target capital **including per-side cost allowances**, not just raw nominal asset exposure. Sensitivities: BTC 35bp vs 60bp buy-side, GLD 15bp buy-side. Round order notional **down to cents**, cash fees **up to cents**, and reduce the proposed notional if even a one-cent fee would breach the target risk sleeve. Historic 35bp is a modeling assumption, not verified trading all-in fee. The BTC amount is **not a valid quantity order** until the account's actual dated min qty, increment, executable ask and jurisdiction/fees are checked. ETFs likewise must have actual customer trade permission.

**Conservative $10 USD BTC minimum example:** at $200.70 in capital the 5% gross-sleeve budget is $10.035 and fees rounded up to $0.04, so a $10.00 BTC buy would cost $10.04, making it **too large**. Minimum in this cents-rounded cash model is **$200.80** at 35bp, and **$201.20** at 60bp. Those are theoretical capital requirements *if and only if* actual BTC minimum really is $10 equivalent. At $130.30 under a fixed $10 minimum, the BTC sleeve cannot be bought; an additional deposit alone cannot change that while original deployable cap stays at $130.30.

**Settlement-aware transition:** for each scenario, report combined actual-dollar budget for buys (BTC, GLD and fees), how much *independently authenticated settled cash* is available, and the dollar shortfall. Since the live account cash is less than $6.51 and existing ETF inventory remains held, no same-run purchase of both sleeves is justified by the funded account evidence. A future autonomous system could decide to sell some of its actual holdings **based on a qualified opportunity strategy**, then wait until the broker verifies the correct spendable settlement before independently opening either new position. Account equity, indicative buying power or a matched sale receipt alone must not be treated as spendable settled proceeds. No **sell** or **buy** occurred in this sizing model.

**Risk/authority boundary:** X4 has no broker access, no secrets, no order writer, no crypto enablement, no authorisation release, and no future money recorded. It stores only fixed-size synthetic tiers. Additional real-world constraints for a larger mandate include portfolio position caps, cash reserve, dynamic target adjustment, fees, slippage, FX/taxes, appropriate venue access, and reliable cross-market order scheduling. Do not gradually raise capital ceiling on observing equity or deposits without separately versioned policy and source-authenticated funds.
