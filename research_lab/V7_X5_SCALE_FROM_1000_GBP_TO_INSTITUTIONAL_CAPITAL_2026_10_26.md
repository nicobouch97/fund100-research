# Fund-100 V7-X5 — investment-scale architecture from a potential £1,000 to institutional-sized capital

**Research-only design, 26 October 2026.** The user expects to contribute **at least £1,000 eventually** and, if investment returns justify it, could increase funding substantially and potentially repeatedly. This statement is an **intention**, *not a deposit, commitment, performance target, guarantee of compounded growth, new capital permission or instruction to trade*. No assumption about the future path of capital is warranted.

## What the fund needs at each scale

- **Initial MICRO size:** test real account-specific broker minimum notional/quantity, spreads, actual settlement and whether even small fractional exposure is feasible; a $130.30 USD maximum principal mandate is currently unchanged.
- **Potential £1k onward:** brokerage currently reports account in USD. The GBP amount **must not be converted using a made-up rate**, and no permission to deploy USD can be inferred before actual broker-credited USD funds, a separately versioned principal mandate and positive compliance/eligibility checks. Funded NAV and *authorised* principal are different sources.
- **Larger balances:** target weights should be generated by evidence-qualified strategies, risk-managed across equities, ETF factors, bonds, gold, crypto and eventual new approved assets (as available), rather than keeping the same 14 ETFs or the historically successful 75% equity/5% BTC/5% gold recipe by default. Never use a return-only optimizer without holding-period, cost, data-quality and risk criteria.
- **Multi-million sizes:** the fraction of an asset’s **real, current traded dollar volume** is crucial. Large trades may require execution over multiple sessions, price impact modelling and different instruments/venues; a simple fixed microaccount maximum-order figure (the existing V6 research has $65 per-order hypothetical cap) cannot be stretched to institutional size unchanged.
- **Potential institutional size:** separate strategy selection and portfolio risk budgets from the implementation layer; use authenticated USD available balances, custody/venue permissions, qualified order min/increments, volume budgets, per-venue capacity, price impact, detailed cost and tax considerations, execution monitoring and reconciled portfolio/settlement journals. This PR does not claim these exist yet.

## What V7-X5 actually implements

A pure **strategy-independent scenario allocator** receiving arbitrarily selected future long-only asset weights, synthetic current holding values, hypothetical USD NAV and candidate *unapproved* principal ceiling, synthetic settled cash, symbolic asset-class metadata, volume and order-size/cost assumptions. The syntax supports multiple equity, ETF, bond ETF, commodity ETF and crypto assets. Stable risk bounds constrain an individual target to at most 50%, combined crypto target to at most 10%, gross investment to at most 95%, and preserve at least a 5% cash target. Those parameters are stress-screen rules, **not an unconditionally optimal allocation or released live policy**.

- Explicit GBP future-funding intention tiers **£1k/£10k/£100k/£1m/£10m** are carried as GBP metadata with **NO conversion to USD**. The USD scenario sizes $130.30/$1k/$10k/$100k/$1m/$10m/$100m are **independent hypothetical stresses**, not estimates of how much GBP has been deposited or what the exchange rate is.
- Fee assumptions are per-asset and cost every simulated buy and sell, with upward cent-rounding **per modeled order ticket**. The modeled minimum order notional and max ticket size are not evidence that Alpaca (or any venue) will accept a trade of that size.
- For every hypothetical difference between held and target assets, compute number of tickets and at least how many **modeled execution days** would be required, using declared unverified 0.5%-of-daily-volume and 30 tickets/day ceilings. **All volume numbers in the regression example are invented stress assumptions**, not sourced ADV, tradable liquidity, or security capacities. At large scale or when a minimum-size condition fails, produce **warnings**; no actual order is created.
- Require synthetic settled cash to cover candidate buys and fees **while maintaining a 5% existing cash reserve**. An associated proposed SELL never becomes spendable buying power within this calculation. No pretended T+1, crypto/ETF cross-calendar conversion, offhours fills or automatic same-run swap.
- Original **$130.30 maximum authorised principal** and HIGH risk account with STOP and engaged kill remain unchanged. Unlike the earlier X4 narrow hypothetical BTC/GLD sleeve sizing, this router works with a future strategy's **different target symbols and weights**. Real source authentication is intentionally NOT implemented; it must be an independent integration before genuine orders.

## What remains before allowing larger autonomous capital

1. **Real strategy evidence and eligibility.** Genuine forward/holdout alpha or persuasive well-specified diversification evidence after fee/impact/tax allowances; per-market operating philosophy; instrument minimum, permission and legal accessibility of the user's particular broker account. Existing BTC/GLD retrospective results are interesting, not statistically demonstrated live outperformance.
2. **Real funding mandate.** Confirm actual broker-posted USD deposits/net credits, authenticated NAV, profit versus contributions and suitable FX conversion source. Separately version the *autonomous investment amount*. User preference is for autonomous decisions under a standing reviewed mandate, **not manual approval of every trade**. The system may not expand a mandate just because the account equity increased.
3. **True execution capacity and position transfers.** Asset-class permission and account size must feed a capacity-aware risk manager using properly timestamped liquidity, cost/spread and order limits, with multi-day trade scheduling, fill/partial-fill/recovery and settled-balance reconciliation before purchases. This PR only provides a deterministic reference calculation.
4. **Gradual exposure of real money proportional to robust performance and loss controls, not a claim of exponential returns.** A larger fund can lose much larger absolute sums even if portfolio percentages remain constant. A high risk profile is not unlimited leverage, crypto concentration, or automatic full investment regardless of edge.

### Explicit exclusions

This is not a live FX calculator, authenticated broker account reading, validated instrument list, order compiler, fund investment recommendation, tax calculator, source of actual market ADV, weekend crash monitor, account credit sensor, or trade release. **No broker GET/POST, no USD FX rate, no new capital authority, no portfolio positions changed.** The existing live $130.30 principal ceiling and engaged STOP/kill remain in effect.
